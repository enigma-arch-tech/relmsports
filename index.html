RELM Sports mission

From local leagues to national-level events, RELM Sports builds opportunities for athletes of every age to grow, compete and have fun. Come and be part of something bigger than the game.

About us Register

Our Story

We got our start back in 2009 and, after a rebrand in 2018, RELM Sports came back stronger. We are a family-run company based in Kingston, Ontario, and our approach is a simple one: give people a great experience through sport and encourage players at every level to come back as often as they can.

RELM stands for Ryan, Erin, Libby and Molly — our family members. We run tournaments, teams, camps and clinics across a range of sports, and we are grateful for everything sport has given us. Sharing those opportunities with your family is what keeps us going. Sport should be fun!

About us

Development

Our leagues are organized, fast-paced and built to challenge players while helping them develop in a competitive environment that stays fun. With divisions grouped by age and skill level, athletes stay sharp, build team chemistry and enjoy the game even more.

Hockey Development Centre

Development here is about more than drills. We focus on mindset, mentorship and long-term growth, with programs built around skill, decision-making and personal development at every level. From first-time skaters to advanced athletes, our coaches help players raise their game on and off the ice.

RELM Hockey Development Centre is a dedicated facility for advancing hockey skills with modern training methods. It is home to two state-of-the-art skatemills in the Fusion Skating & Shooting Zone, giving athletes an exceptional place to sharpen skating, shooting and overall performance. HDC Kingston is where players go when they want to reach the next level.
