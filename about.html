About us

  • Home
  • About us

RELM Sports is a family-run company in Kingston, Ontario that delivers tournaments, teams, leagues, camps and clinics. We work with athletes of all ages and skill levels, and we build every program around the same idea: sport should be a great experience worth coming back to.

We have been involved in sport our whole lives, and that is exactly why we do this. Whether a player is lacing up for the first time or chasing a spot at the next level, our job is to give them a place to grow, compete and enjoy the game. We are proud of the community that has grown around RELM Sports and grateful to every family that has been part of it.

What we stand for

Our Values

Fun First

If athletes enjoy what they are doing, they keep coming back. Everything we plan starts with that in mind.

Real Development

Purposeful coaching, measurable progress and a pathway that makes sense whatever level a player is at.

Family & Community

We are a family business and we run our events the way we would want our own family treated.

Integrity

Clear communication, fair competition and organizers who follow through on what they promise.

Our Story

We began in 2009, and after a rebrand in 2018 we relaunched as RELM Sports. The name is made up of our family members' initials — Ryan, Erin, Libby and Molly — which says a lot about how we operate.

Today that family approach stretches across a training centre, travel teams, house leagues, tournaments and camps. We are grateful for how much sport has given us, and we love being able to pass those opportunities on to your family.

The people behind it

our team

Our staff blends tournament organizers, league coordinators, certified coaches and training centre specialists. A small core team runs day-to-day operations, supported by coaches and volunteers who make every event happen.
